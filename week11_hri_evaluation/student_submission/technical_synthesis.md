For routine handover I would choose autonomous mode. It was fastest, at 58.3 s with 2 inputs, against 97.8 s / 4 inputs for voice and 126.9 s / 165 for manual.
When the person's needs may change mid-task, I would choose voice shared mode.  Autonomous was quicker in the changed-goal trial (64.5 s, 4 inputs), but only because I knew to press STOP and restart with red. With voice I changed the goal by speaking, and the guards kept it safe. “Bring me the red cup” at 82 s was blocked (hold), and only “actually bring me the red cup” at 136.7 s moved the robot to CONFIRM, so the robot never switched goals without confirmation. The cost was time (218.8 s), because the feedback didn’t say which phrase was needed. Manual was worst: 537 s and 271 inputs, including 14 refused Offer attempts while docking.
Redesign: in the baseline, I answered “Which cup do you mean, red or blue?” with “blue”, which was heard as “no”, and reaching CONFIRM took 56.6 s and 3 attempts. With the revised prompt “Say ‘the red cup’ or ‘the blue cup’”, my answer “the blue cup” at 18.85 s was accepted on the first try
